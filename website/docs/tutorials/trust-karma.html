Trust & Reputation

8 min read

How KARMA works, explained simply

Learn how nodes build trust in YAKMESH—no math required. Understand reputation tiers and why trust matters for a healthy network.

1 What Is Reputation?

Think of reputation like your credit score, but for network trust. Instead of measuring how well you pay bills, it measures how reliably your node behaves on the network.

Real-world analogy: Imagine moving to a new neighborhood. At first, nobody knows you. Over time, as you help neighbors, keep your lawn tidy, and show up to community events, people start trusting you more. That's exactly how KARMA works.

Your node's reputation is earned, not given. New nodes start with minimal trust, and over time—by being reliable, responding correctly, and not misbehaving—they climb the trust ladder.

2 How Do You Earn Reputation?

KARMA tracks multiple signals to calculate your trustworthiness:

  • ✓
    Time online — The longer your node has been running consistently, the more trust you build. Patience pays off.
  • ✓
    Correct responses — When other nodes ask for data, do you deliver valid content? Accuracy builds trust.
  • ✓
    Good timing — Does your node keep good time? Nodes with accurate clocks (especially hardware clocks like GPS) score higher.
  • ✓
    Peer endorsements — When trusted nodes vouch for you, that matters. It's like a job reference.
  • ✓
    Hardware verification — Modern CPUs have security features. Using them proves you're running real software, not a fake.

3 Trust Tiers: The Ladder of Trust

Every node falls into one of these tiers. Think of them as levels in a video game— you start at Level 0 and work your way up.

🌱 NAYA (New Node)

Level 0

"The newcomer" — Just joined the network.

  • • Can browse and request content
  • • Limited ability to serve content to others
  • • Other nodes watch your behavior carefully

🥈 YATRI (Established)

Level 1

"The traveler" — Been around for a few days, behaving well.

  • • 1+ day of consistent uptime
  • • Hardware security features verified
  • • Can serve content to other nodes

💎 SATHI (Trusted)

Level 3

"The companion" — A reliable member of the network.

  • • 14+ days of consistent uptime
  • • GPS or high-quality time source
  • • Endorsed by higher-tier nodes
  • • Priority access to network resources

🏔️ SIRDAR (Elder)

Level 4

"The expedition leader" — The most trusted nodes on the network.

  • • 30+ days of consistent uptime
  • • Atomic or GPS time source
  • • Full hardware attestation
  • • Can endorse other nodes
  • • Helps make network-wide decisions

4 Why Trust Matters for Content Delivery

Trust isn't just about feeling good—it has real practical effects:

  • 🚀
    Faster routing — High-trust nodes get priority when routing content. Your files arrive faster.
  • 🛡️
    Better security — You receive content from trusted sources, reducing the risk of corrupted or malicious data.
  • 🌐
    Network stability — Trusted nodes form the backbone of the mesh. They're the reliable infrastructure everyone depends on.
  • ⚖️
    Fair resource allocation — Limited resources (like bandwidth) go to nodes that have proven they're good citizens.

5 How Bad Actors Are Detected and Isolated

Not everyone plays nice. Here's how YAKMESH protects itself:

What gets you in trouble:

  • ✗ Serving bad data — Content that doesn't match its hash
  • ✗ Going offline often — Unreliable availability
  • ✗ Bad timing — Clock way off from the network
  • ✗ Spam behavior — Sending too many requests or fake messages
  • ✗ Eclipse attacks — Trying to isolate nodes from the network

What happens to bad actors:

  1. 1. Warning — First offense might just reduce your score slightly
  2. 2. Demotion — Keep misbehaving and you drop to a lower tier
  3. 3. Isolation — Serious offenders get disconnected from trusted nodes
  4. 4. Blacklist — The worst actors are blocked entirely
Important: Reputation is hard to earn and easy to lose. A single serious offense can undo weeks of good behavior. Play nice!

6 Check Your Reputation

Want to see where you stand? Use these commands: 

# See your current karma tier and score
npx yakmesh karma status

# View detailed karma breakdown
npx yakmesh karma details

# See what your peers think of you
npx yakmesh karma endorsements

You can also check your Dashboard at http://localhost:9526 to see your trust score in real-time.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • ✓ Reputation is earned through consistent good behavior over time
  • ✓ Trust tiers range from NAYA (new) to SIRDAR (elder)
  • ✓ Higher trust = faster routing, better security, more privileges
  • ✓ Bad actors are automatically detected and isolated
  • ✓ Keep your node online, accurate, and honest to build trust
