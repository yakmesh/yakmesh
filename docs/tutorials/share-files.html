Share Files with Friends

Learn to share files directly from your device - no cloud uploads required

Beginner ⏱️ 5 min read 📁 DOKO Protocol

What You'll Learn

In this tutorial, you'll learn how to share files and folders with friends using YAKMESH's DOKO (Distributed Object Keeper and Organizer) system. Unlike traditional file sharing services, YAKMESH doesn't upload your files to any cloud server. Instead, your files stay on your device and are shared directly with your friends through an encrypted peer-to-peer connection.

  • Share a folder from your computer
  • Generate a shareable link
  • Send the link to a friend
  • Understand how DOKO links work

📋 Before You Start

Make sure you have:

  • YAKMESH installed and running on your computer
  • Your node online and connected to the mesh (check your dashboard)
  • A folder or file you want to share

💡 Tip: If you haven't set up YAKMESH yet, check out the Getting Started guide first.

1 Choose What to Share

First, decide which folder or file you want to share. For this tutorial, let's say you have a folder called vacation-photos in your Documents folder that you want to share with a friend.

Open your terminal or command prompt and navigate to the location of your files: 

# On Windows
cd C:\Users\YourName\Documents

# On macOS/Linux
cd ~/Documents

Verify your folder is there: 

# List contents
ls -la vacation-photos/

2 Share the Folder with YAKMESH

Now, use the yakmesh share command to create a shareable link for your folder: 

# Share the entire folder
yakmesh share vacation-photos/

You'll see output like this: 

📤 Sharing: vacation-photos/
   Files: 47 items (234.5 MB total)
   
✅ Share created successfully!

🔗 Share Link:
   yak://qubit-lattice-prism/vacation-photos

📋 Or use the compact iO code:
   qubit-lattice-prism

⏰ Share expires: Never (persistent)
🔒 Encryption: End-to-end encrypted

ℹ️ What happened? YAKMESH indexed your files, created a cryptographic content fingerprint, and generated a human-readable iO name (like "qubit-lattice-prism") that's easy to share verbally. Your files stay on your device - nothing is uploaded anywhere.

💡 Why iO names? Instead of ugly hashes like "QmX7k2n...", YAKMESH uses a 256-word quantum-themed wordlist to create memorable 3-word codes. Each word encodes 8 bits of the content fingerprint, giving you 24 bits of uniqueness - enough for millions of distinct shares.

3 Customize Your Share (Optional)

The basic share command works great, but you can customize it with additional options:

Set an Expiration Time

# Share expires in 7 days
yakmesh share vacation-photos/ --expires 7d

# Share expires in 24 hours
yakmesh share vacation-photos/ --expires 24h

Limit Number of Downloads

# Only allow 3 downloads
yakmesh share vacation-photos/ --max-downloads 3

Password Protect the Share

# Require a password to access
yakmesh share vacation-photos/ --password "secretcode123"

Combine Options

# Expires in 2 days, max 5 downloads, password protected
yakmesh share vacation-photos/ --expires 2d --max-downloads 5 --password "familytrip"

4 Send the Link to Your Friend

Copy the share link and send it to your friend using any messaging app, email, or however you prefer. There are two formats you can use:

Full yak:// Link

yak://qubit-lattice-prism/vacation-photos

This link can be clicked if your friend has YAKMESH installed - it opens the download automatically. The human-readable words ("qubit-lattice-prism") uniquely identify your content.

iO Code (for verbal sharing)

qubit-lattice-prism

Just tell your friend these three words! They can fetch it with: 

yakmesh fetch qubit-lattice-prism

No typing long hashes - just memorable quantum words.

⚠️ Important: Your computer must be online and running YAKMESH for your friend to download the files. If you shut down your computer, the share will be temporarily unavailable until you come back online.

5 Your Friend Downloads the Files

When your friend receives the link, they can download the files using these methods:

Method A: Click the Link

If they have YAKMESH installed, clicking the yakmesh:// link will open the download interface automatically.

Method B: Use the Command Line

# Using the iO code (easy to share verbally!)
yakmesh fetch qubit-lattice-prism

# Using the full yak:// link
yakmesh fetch "yak://qubit-lattice-prism/vacation-photos"

# Download to a specific folder
yakmesh fetch qubit-lattice-prism --output ~/Downloads/vacation-pics/

Method C: Use the Dashboard

Open the YAKMESH dashboard, click "Receive Files", and paste the share link or code.

6 Manage Your Active Shares

You can view and manage all your active shares using the yakmesh doko commands:

List All Active Shares

yakmesh share list

Output example:

📋 Your Active Shares:

iO Name              Description        Size      Downloads  Expires
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
qubit-lattice-prism  vacation-photos    234.5 MB  2/∞        Never
photon-cipher-node   work-documents     12.3 MB   0/5        2d 14h
fermion-hash-crystal birthday-video     1.2 GB    1/1        Expired

Total: 3 shares (1.45 GB)

Get Details About a Specific Share

yakmesh share info qubit-lattice-prism

Stop Sharing

# Stop a specific share
yakmesh share remove qubit-lattice-prism

# Stop all shares
yakmesh share clear --all

Extend Expiration

# Add 7 more days to an expiring share
yakmesh share extend photon-cipher-node --add 7d

🔍 How DOKO Links Work

Understanding what happens behind the scenes helps you use YAKMESH more effectively:

  1. Content Fingerprinting: When you share a folder, YAKMESH creates a unique cryptographic fingerprint of your content. This is then converted to a human-readable iO name (like "qubit-lattice-prism") that you can easily share verbally.
  2. Mesh Registration: Your node announces to the mesh network that it has this content available. Other nodes record this information but don't download the actual files.
  3. Direct Connection: When your friend requests the files, their node finds yours on the mesh and establishes a direct encrypted connection. No middleman servers are involved.
  4. Chunk Transfer: Files are sent in small encrypted chunks. If the connection is interrupted, the download can resume where it left off.
  5. Verification: Your friend's node verifies that the received content matches the fingerprint in the iO name, ensuring nothing was corrupted or tampered with during transfer.

💡 Privacy Note: Only the content hash is visible on the mesh network. File names, folder structure, and actual content are encrypted and only accessible to someone with the share link.

🔧 Troubleshooting

"Share not found" error

This usually means your node is offline. Check that YAKMESH is running with yakmesh status.

Slow download speeds

This depends on your internet upload speed. For large files, consider using --replicate to cache copies on other nodes.

Friend can't connect

Check that both of you are connected to the mesh network. Run yakmesh peers to see your connections.

Share expired accidentally

You can recreate it with the same command. The content hash will be identical since the files haven't changed.

🎉 Summary

Congratulations! You've learned how to share files with friends using YAKMESH. Here's a quick recap:

  • yakmesh share [path] - Create a share link
  • yakmesh doko list - View your active shares
  • yakmesh fetch [link] - Download shared content
  • Files stay on your device - no cloud uploads!
  • All transfers are end-to-end encrypted
